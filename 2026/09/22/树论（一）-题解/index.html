∑i=1nd(i)≈O(nlog⁡n)\sum^{n}_{i=1} d(i) \approx O(n\log n) i=1∑n​d(i)≈O(nlogn)

为何？首先显然 ∑i=1nd(i)=∑i=1n⌊n/i⌋\sum^{n}_{i=1} d(i) = \sum_{i=1}^{n}\lfloor n/i \rfloor∑i=1n​d(i)=∑i=1n​⌊n/i⌋
